## 2. Setup

- **Grids.** $101\times102^2$ and $201\times204^2$ (the M3 pair, $\beta$ off-node). $V(\cdot,\tau)$ snapshots every 0.5 in $\tau$.
- **Mask.** $R\ge0.45$ throughout.
- **Read.** Each point at retrograde time $\tau$ is read at the first snapshot with $\tau_k\ge\tau$ (`M4_READ=ceil`). $V$ is non-increasing in the horizon, and on the active barrier $V(x,T)=0$ for every $T\ge\tau$. Reading below $\tau$ biases $V$ outward (explainer §3).
- **Tolerance.** Each grid's own one-cell value change: 0.118 on the coarse grid and 0.059 on the fine. It is not pinned.
- **Bundle.** 122 Layer 0 trajectories, regenerated from `test_barrier.py`'s own seeds. They reproduce their invariants: $\max|FI-1|=1.1\times10^{-10}$, $\max|H^*|=1.4\times10^{-10}$.

## 3. Results

### 3.1 Test A — level (tested trajectories only)

| family | tested / n | median length, 101 | median length, 201 | departure side, 101 / 201 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| off-boresight | 64 / 66 | **1.69** (max 4.20) | **1.62** (max 4.17) | 36 in, 28 out / 36 in, 28 out |
| min-range | 20 / 48 | 0.26 | 0.18 | 18 in, 2 never left / 20 in |
| max-range | 8 / 8 | 0.20 | 0.16 | 8 out / 8 out |

- **Excluded trajectories.** 30 trajectories have no point above the mask: 28 min-range and 2 off-boresight. They are excluded, not counted as agreeing.
- **Off-boresight.** The agreement length is essentially grid-independent, even though the fine grid's tolerance is half the coarse grid's.
- **BUP seeds.** All 80 masked seeds interpolate to $\ell=0$ on the $\tau=0$ snapshot: $2.1\times10^{-2}$ on the coarse grid and $1.2\times10^{-2}$ on the fine. This checks $\ell$, not the solve.
- **Converged $V$ at the seeds** (report only). The median is about 0, but the minimum is $-0.58$, so some BUP points lie well inside the converged winning zone. Those seeds are not on the global barrier.

**Max-range.** Every trajectory departs shortly after crossing $\phi_2=0$, the kink of
$\bar R(\phi_2)$.

| seed $\phi_2$ | crosses $\phi_2=0$ at $\tau$ | departs, 101 | departs, 201 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\pm8.86°$ | 0.157 / 0.166 | 0.225 / 0.250 | 0.200 / 0.225 |
| $\pm4.43°$ | 0.078 / 0.085 | 0.150 / 0.175 | 0.125 / 0.125 |

Departure minus crossing is 0.068–0.090 on the coarse grid and 0.040–0.059 on the fine. The lag
shrinks with the tolerance, so the departure is tied to the crossing.

## 4. Findings

### 4.1 The off-boresight barrier is corroborated

- **Two routes agree.** Layer 0 reaches the barrier by backward characteristics and Layer 1 by a
  global PDE. They agree in level, direction and control over a median of about 1.65 in $\tau$
  (the longest stretch is 4.2).
- **The agreement survives refinement.** The direction error drops by a factor of 4 under
  $2\times$ refinement.

### 4.2 Max-range: the departure is the correct answer

The max-range sheets do not bound any volume the grid can resolve.

- **They peel off at the ridge.** Each trajectory stays within about $2\times10^{-3}$ of its own
  face until it crosses $\phi_2=0$, then rises over the opposite face.
- **They enclose nothing.** The sheets from the two BUP lenses run away from each other.
- **The grid agrees.** Past the crossing, $V=\ell$: player 1 cannot improve on the margin he
  already has.
- **The closing pieces are below resolution.** Layer 0's corner work (`claude/Layer0_Handoff_2026-09-21.md`) puts every closing piece within about $2.2\times10^{-3}$ of the ridge, 30–60 times below a grid cell.

D&S's two small max-range zones lie on the target set itself, as the usable part of the max-range
face. These departures are **not** candidate junctions.

### 4.3 The switch junctions, and D&S's commuting line $S_1U_1$

**$S_1U_1$ is reconstructed to Table 2 precision** (`layer0/viz/commuting_s1u1.py`, run 2026-10-07).
In Table 3 it is the pursuer's commuting line, with strategy $(0,-1)$.

- **$S_1$ is a stagnation point.** Table 2's $S_1=(1.468,\ 47.24°,\ 132.76°)$ is a $(-1,-1)$
  stagnation point to table precision: $R-2\sin\phi_1=-4\times10^{-4}$ and $\phi_1+\phi_2=\pi$.
- **The line from $S_1$ lands on $U_1$.** Flown from $S_1$ under $(0,-1)$ with the singular costate
  $\lambda=(\cos\phi_1,\ 0,\ -R\sin\phi_1)$, it passes Table 2's $U_1=(1.721,\ 91.14°,\ 134.99°)$
  at $3.6\times10^{-4}$ ($\Delta R=+0.0003$, $\Delta\phi_1=-0.007°$, $\Delta\phi_2=+0.009°$).
- **The construction is exact.** Every invariant holds to $10^{-13}$ or better.
- **The reverse check agrees.** Searching the stagnation curve for the line through $U_1$
  recovers $S_1$'s $\phi_1$ as $47.238°$, against Table 2's $47.24°$.

So $S_1U_1$ is a reconstructed Table 3 line, and $S_1$ and $U_1$ are placed Table 2 points.

**M4's switch points are not on that line:**

- **Where they sit.** All 80 Layer 0 $\lambda_1$ switches in the off-boresight family lie on the
  planes $\phi_2=\pm135.00°$. Under $(-1,-1)$, $\dot\phi_1=\dot\phi_2$, so each trajectory reaches
  the plane at a $\phi_1$ fixed by its seed.
- **How far off they are.** The 16 inside retained segments sit 0.04–0.11 from $S_1U_1$ (state
  distance in $R$ and radians). That is 100–300 times the line's miss at $U_1$.
- **They cross rather than touch.** $\dot\lambda_1$ there runs from $+0.07$ to $+0.72$. A closed
  $(-1,-1)$ barrier orbit can only *touch* $\lambda_1=0$; it cannot cross.
- **They meet the line at one point, $U_1$.** Along the plane, $\dot\lambda_1$ changes sign between
  $\phi_1=86°$ and $92°$, so the crossing becomes a touch at about $U_1$ ($91.14°$). That is the one
  point where Layer 0's switch locus and the commuting line meet.
- **A lead worth checking.** The switch nearest $U_1$ (0.022 away, $\dot\lambda_1=-0.04$) comes
  from the seed at $|\phi_2|=87.9°$, beside Table 2's $J_1$ ($\phi_2=88.87°$). D&S's outermost closed
  orbit is $J_1E_1J_1$.

**What stands and what changes:**
- **They are still junctions.** The grid follows Layer 0's control up to each switch, and each
  trajectory leaves inward afterward.
- **They are not a Table 3 line.** Table 3 lists no pursuer switch line here: its four pursuer
  switch lines all sit at $R<0.6$. That fits D&S's closed orbits never switching in this region.
- **The grid's reading.** Layer 0's post-switch segments are characteristics outside D&S's
  structure, and the grid marks them as interior.

### 4.4 Min-range is unconfirmed

- **Most of it is out of reach.** 28 of the 48 trajectories lie entirely below the mask.
- **The rest leave at once.** The 20 testable ones leave $\{V=0\}$ almost immediately, inward,
  next to the inner face M3 found leaky.
- **Status.** Deprioritized.

## 7. Reproducing

Run from `layer1/`:

```
python test_m4.py 101x102_T6     # about 1 min
python test_m4.py 201x204_T6     # about 4 min
```

**Environment switches:**
- `M4_READ=conv` reads the converged $V$ everywhere and gives the same conclusions;
- `M4_TOL` pins the tolerance across grids;
- `R_MARGIN` sets the mask (default 0.45);
- `LAYER1_ELL_SCALING` selects the $\ell$ weighting (`raw`, `plan` or `sdist`; this run used `sdist`).

**Files:**
- `layer1/test_m4.py`, `layer1/l0_traj.py`, `layer1/hji.py`, `layer1/ell.py`;
- `layer0/barrier.py`, `layer0/test_barrier.py`, `layer0/validate_table2.py`;
- the M3 snapshot stacks `layer1/m3_out/Vsnap_<grid>_T6.npz`.

`test_m4.py` evaluates one snapshot at a time to bound memory. Holding all interpolators at once
at $201\times204^2$ needs about 1.9 GB and is killed.
