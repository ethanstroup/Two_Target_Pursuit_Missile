Layer 1 — M1: ℓ(x), the implicit surface function of T₁→₂

ℓ = max(|φ₁| − β, R_lo(φ₂) − R, R − R_hi(φ₂)), raw (unweighted) form, evaluated fresh in this page. Negative inside T₁→₂, zero on its boundary, positive outside. ℓ has no τ: it is the static boundary condition V(x, 0) = ℓ(x) that M3 marches from. The surface is marching cubes at ℓ = 0 on a regular grid; the dark lines are the closed-form edges of T₁ (R_lo, R_hi at φ₁ = ±β), drawn independently, so the two can be checked against each other.
