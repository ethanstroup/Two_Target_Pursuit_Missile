Layer 0 — the barrier as a swept surface

The boundary of the usable part is a curve. Every point on it is a terminal condition: state on the target-set boundary, costate from transversality (Eq. 20). Integrating the coupled state–costate system backward under σ₁* = −sign λ₁, σ₂* = +sign λ₂ gives one barrier trajectory per point, and the bundle of them is the semipermeable surface. Scrub τ to sweep it. Maths comes from layer0/barrier.js; the invariants |FI−1| and |H*| below are the integration's own error certificate.
