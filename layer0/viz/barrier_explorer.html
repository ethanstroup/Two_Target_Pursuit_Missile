From terminal states to a surface

The boundary of the usable part is a curve. Every point on it is a terminal condition: state on the target-set boundary, costate from transversality (Eq. 20). Integrating the coupled state–costate system backward under σ₁* = −sign λ₁, σ₂* = +sign λ₂ gives one barrier trajectory per point, and the bundle of them is the semipermeable surface. At τ = 0 only the (BUP) is drawn, over the target-set boundaries and the Table 2 points; raise τ to sweep the sheet.

These are single-target barrier families. They do not yet form the complete two-target outcome partition.
